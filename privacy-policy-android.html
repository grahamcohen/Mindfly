Mindfly Privacy Policy (Android)

Last Updated: November 2026

This policy covers the Android version of Mindfly, distributed through Google Play. The iPhone version has its own privacy policy.

Overview

Mindfly ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile game developed by Graham Cohen. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Data Collection

Mindfly does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or other personal files
  • We do not show ads or use analytics in the Android version
  • We do not share any data with third parties for marketing purposes

Local Data Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Game progress (high scores, unlocked items, Flight School completion)
  • Game settings (language, sensitivity, vibration, display options)
  • In-app purchase status

We cannot see this data. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account.

Camera and Face Tracking

Mindfly uses your front camera so you can steer with your head. Camera frames are processed entirely on your device by Google's MediaPipe Face Landmarker, which runs inside the app. During a flight the app reads your head angle and a few facial expression values (mouth open, cheek puff, smile, and raised eyebrows) to move your character and trigger in-game abilities. This information is:

  • Processed entirely on your device
  • Held in temporary memory only while the camera is in use
  • Never recorded, written to storage, or transmitted to any server or third party
  • Never used for facial recognition, identification, or authentication

The camera preview on the calibration screen is only shown on your screen and is never saved.

Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play and are subject to the Google Privacy Policy. The app only receives a confirmation of which item you bought so it can deliver it, and keeps that confirmation on your device.

Google Play Games Services

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your Play Games profile, scores, and achievements are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Your gamer name and high score may appear on the Mindfly leaderboard, depending on your Play Games settings.

Children's Privacy

Mindfly does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.

Contact Us
